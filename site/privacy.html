Kuchi privacy

Updated November 12, 2026. Kuchi is Kalaris Labs’ collection of launch-video references.

No accounts

Kuchi has no accounts and no sign-in. You can browse the collection, play videos and connect an agent to the MCP server without giving us your name, email address or any other account information. Kuchi does not accept uploads.

Your agent and service providers

Agents connect to Kuchi’s MCP server without credentials. Its tools return reference videos and their metadata only. Your agent provider handles your conversations under its own privacy policy; Kuchi does not receive them.

Cloudflare hosts the website, the videos and the MCP service. It processes request information, including IP addresses, to deliver the service and prevent abuse (requests are rate-limited per IP address). Operational logs may include request times, paths and errors.

Product analytics

When enabled, we use PostHog in the United States to understand homepage and collection visits, reference viewing, filter use, connection attempts and MCP request status. Random browser and session identifiers are stored locally to recognize returning browsers and group visits, with a new session after 30 minutes of inactivity. We record a coarse browser or known-automation category without storing the user-agent string.

We do not record sessions, page text, search text, product prompts or email addresses in analytics. IP address capture is disabled for this project. Browser analytics respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control. You can clear the site’s local storage to remove its browser identifier.
